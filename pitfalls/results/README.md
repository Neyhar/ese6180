# Current Pitfalls results

Start with [the focused experiment](coupled_higher_dimensions/README.md).

- [History and one-action control](coupled_higher_dimensions/figures/overview_history.png)
- [Execution length at sufficient history](coupled_higher_dimensions/figures/overview_execution.png)

These plots use only initial changes in x1, with dimensions 2, 4, 8, and 16,
five training seeds, and 40 test conditions per seed. The lines show means,
without shading. Noisy/noiseless refer to training-data collection; all testing
is noiseless after the initial change.

The result folder preserves raw data and full source snapshots. Its optional
exploratory results and separately labeled original-paper n=2 reference remain
available, but are not mixed into the two main comparisons.

Earlier studies moved together with their original runners to
[archive/pitfalls-before-cleanup/](../../archive/pitfalls-before-cleanup/README.md).
Their seed grids and protocols differ; they are not additional independent
replications of the current study.
